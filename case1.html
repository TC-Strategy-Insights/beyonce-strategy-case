Executive
Summary

•

The Challenge: Cities treated mega-concerts as entertainment bookings, with no numbers to justify spending on transit or hotels.

•

The Action: Mapped the three ways money moves through a host city: direct spending, business revenue, and the jobs that follow.

•

The Result: A projected $4.5 billion in U.S. economic activity, most of it landing outside the venue.

The Strategic Arc

The Challenge

  • Mega-concerts create cultural buzz but lack quantifiable ROI data. Tourism boards needed defensible metrics to justify city-wide infrastructure investments and permit approvals for stadium tours.

The Strategy

  • Methodology:
  • • Direct Impact: Ticket Sales & Merch.
  • • Indirect Impact: Hotel Occupancy & Dining.
  • • Induced Impact: Long-term Job Creation.
  • • Analysis: Compared hotel revenue on concert dates against normal dates in the same season.
