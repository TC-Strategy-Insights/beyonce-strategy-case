Core Insights:

  • Commercial operators (6+ listings): 10,081 listings — a third of all supply — priced at a $209 median, above the citywide $173. Scale is being used to command a premium, not to discount.

Insight 01: The Summer Spike (Overlap):

Supply is concentrated: Manhattan and Brooklyn hold 80% of all listings (24,271 of 30,259), making them the only two boroughs where short-term rentals meaningfully pressure hotel demand.

Insight 02: The Quality Trade-off:

Hosts with 6+ properties command a $209 median rate — 21% above the citywide $173. Professional operators are not competing on price; they are capturing premium demand.

✓ RECOMMENDATION: Treat the top host tier as the true competitive set. Benchmark service and pricing against professional operators in Manhattan and Brooklyn, not against the long tail of single-listing hosts.
